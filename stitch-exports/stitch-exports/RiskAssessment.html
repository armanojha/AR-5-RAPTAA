Statutory & Methodological Notice · National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP)

The Civitrace Composite Risk Score (CRS) is an algorithmic heuristic synthesized from multispectral satellite observations, synthetic aperture radar (InSAR) coherence matrices, contractor billing telemetry, and public milestone milestones. This metric is designed strictly to prioritize federal engineering inspection resources, optimize Joint Physical Verification (JPV) rosters, and flag early-stage drift. It does not constitute a legal determination of contractual breach or financial culpability.
